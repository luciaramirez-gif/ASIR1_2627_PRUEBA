About our Interviewer & Host

Adam El Ouazizi

To begin with, Adam is a graduate in Audiovisual Communication and Digital Journalism. He started his career reading news bulletins at a local radio station, and in addition, he later specialized in creating tech content for social media. In my view, his natural charisma and ability to guide debates are what make him such a great host. The reason why he created TechTalk is that he wanted a fresh format to explain complex technology to young people. Therefore, he needed a dynamic co-host with whom he had great chemistry, so he pitched the idea to Lucía, his former university classmate. In a nutshell, they decided to team up and invite Victoria to bring technical depth to the show.

About our Principal Interviewer

Lucía Ramírez

First of all, Lucía holds a degree in Sociology and a Master's in Digital Culture and Social Media. She worked for three years as a digital trend analyst and community manager for music platforms and cultural brands. Actually, she has always been fascinated by how technology shapes human emotions and identity. As a result, when Adam launched TechTalk, he definitely thought that Lucía was the ideal person to provide the human perspective against cold data. Furthermore, she was the one who suggested contacting her old high school friend, Victoria, after reading her articles on online privacy. To put it simply, Lucía acts as the perfect bridge between the audience's feelings and the tech world.

About our Expert & Guest

Victoria Sánchez

Speaking in general terms, Victoria is a Computer Engineer who specialized in Cybersecurity and Data Protection. She initially worked as a software developer, however, she soon transitioned into digital privacy consulting, advising companies on how to protect user information. Without doubt, her legal and technical knowledge makes her an authority on the subject. She stayed in touch with Lucía since high school and often helped her with online security doubts. Consequently, when Adam and Lucía were preparing the episode about digital legacy, they immediately thought of Victoria to explain the legal side of tech platforms. All in all, her expertise completes the ideal trio for the podcast
