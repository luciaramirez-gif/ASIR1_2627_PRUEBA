Welcome to TechTalk: Introduction

Our studio, Madrid City Center • November 16, 2026 • 5:00 pm

Welcome to our first episode! We created this podcast to explore a fascinating yet overlooked question: what happens—or doesn't happen—to our personal data and digital accounts when we pass away?

From Netflix profiles and Spotify playlists to locked iCloud memories, most people take their digital footprint for granted. Through this open debate, we uncover the hidden legal, technical, and emotional world behind our digital legacy.
