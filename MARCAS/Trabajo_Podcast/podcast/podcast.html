Behind the Script

Writing the original script took us several days of brainstorming, drafting, and careful revision. We wanted every transition, sound cue, and dialogue to feel like a natural, engaging conversation rather than a stiff reading.

After adjusting the timing and refining our ideas together, we finally achieved our goal: a well-balanced, dynamic episode that clearly explores our digital legacy.

00:00 - 01:45

1. Introduction

Hosts Adam and Lucía open the episode by asking what happens to our Netflix, Spotify, and Instagram accounts after we die. They introduce the three main topics and welcome tech privacy guest Victoria.

01:45 - 04:30

2. Block One: Digital Legacy & Tech Policies

Nobody reads terms and conditions. We discuss locked iCloud accounts, strict company privacy policies, and where to draw the line between sharing family photos and keeping private DMs secret.

04:30 - 07:15

3. Block Two: The Digital Ghost Phenomenon

What happens when social media algorithms suggest profiles or birthday reminders of people who passed away? We debate the emotional impact versus Facebook's "Legacy Contact" memorial feature.

07:15 - 09:30

4. Block Three: Emotional vs. Practical Value

Bank accounts hold financial value, while curated Spotify playlists, photos, and memes carry deep personal meaning. Victoria shares practical advice on organizing a digital will proactively.

09:30 - 10:15

5. Outro and Goodbyes

Lucía and Adam wrap up the conversation and thank Victoria for her insights. They invite listeners to comment on what digital memories they would leave behind before signing off until the next episode.
