Studio Bloopers

Recording in English was a fun challenge! We had hilarious moments trying to pronounce complex technical terms without laughing or stumbling over the microphone.

Honestly, recording these takes was harder than expected because we simply couldn't stop laughing! Have a listen to some of our funniest outtakes:

Hilarious Recording Anecdotes

1. When the Universe Turned Against Us

We started recording sheltered under a building overhang to avoid the wind and rain. Out of nowhere, a massive downpour began, and it was hilarious how it genuinely felt as if the whole universe had turned against us!

2. A Million Retakes Thanks to Victoria

We had to repeat our lines what felt like a million times because Victoria kept getting tongue-tied. Every time she stumbled over a word, the rest of us burst out laughing, making it almost impossible to finish a single take.

3. The Unexpected Children's Academy

Once we had finally recorded a huge portion of the episode, we realized we were standing right next to a children's academy. Suddenly, dozens of kids came out making a ton of noise, forcing us to scrap everything and start from scratch.
